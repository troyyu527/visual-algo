Quick Sort

Quick sort (or partition-exchange sort) is an efficient divide-and-conquer sort — typically faster than merge sort and heap sort on random data.

Each round picks a pivot and partitions the range around it: everything smaller than the pivot is moved before it and everything larger after it, which drops the pivot into its final sorted position. Quick sort then applies itself to the two sides.

Complexity

  • Best & average case: O(n log n)
  • Worst case: O(n²) — poor pivots
  • Space: O(log n)
