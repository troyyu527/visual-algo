Heap

A heap is a tree-based structure that keeps its most extreme value instantly reachable at the root. It is an almost-complete binary tree that obeys the heap property:

  • Max heap: every parent is greater than or equal to its children, so the maximum sits at the root.
  • Min heap: every parent is less than or equal to its children, so the minimum sits at the root.

Operations

  • Insertion: add the element at the next free spot, then sift up until the heap property holds again.
  • Extraction: remove the root, move the last element into its place, then sift down.
  • Replacement: overwrite the root with a new value and sift down — one pass instead of an extract plus an insert.

Actions

  • Heapify: turn an arbitrary array into a valid heap, bottom-up.
  • Sift down / Sift up: restore the heap property by swapping a value with the larger of its two children as it moves down (max heap), or with its parent as it moves up.
